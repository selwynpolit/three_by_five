# Snooze and Hide

Sometimes a card is perfectly valid — it's just not for right now. Maybe you're waiting on someone, or the tasks on it can't start until tomorrow, or you just need it out of your sight for a few hours. That's what snooze and hide are for.

They're two distinct tools, so here's the difference in plain terms: **hide** puts a card away indefinitely until you decide to bring it back. **Snooze** puts a card away until a specific time, after which it automatically reappears. Both are reversible. Neither deletes anything.

---

## Hide

Right-click any card and choose "Hide". The card disappears from your Card View immediately. It's still in your system — it's just out of the way.

A toast notification appears at the bottom of the screen for four seconds after you hide a card. If you changed your mind, click Undo right then and the card comes straight back.

To see all your hidden cards, look for the eye icon in the card view header and toggle it on. Hidden (and snoozed) cards reappear, shown slightly faded so you can tell them apart from your active cards. Right-click any of them and choose "Unhide" to restore it permanently.

**Good for:** Cards you've finished with but aren't ready to archive. Cards for projects that are on hold indefinitely. Anything you want to tuck away without making a decision about.

---

## Snooze

Right-click any card and choose "Snooze until…". A small menu gives you these options:

- **2 hours** — back before your next check-in
- **Tonight (8 PM)** — done with it for the workday
- **Tomorrow morning (8 AM)** — a clean start to a new day
- **For a week** — put it away, come back fresh
- **Pick a date** — set any specific date you want

The card disappears from view and comes back automatically when the snooze expires. You don't have to remember it — 3by5 checks for expired snoozes every 60 seconds, even when the app is running in the background.

Like hiding, a toast appears after you snooze a card. Click Undo within four seconds to cancel the snooze and bring the card right back.

**Good for:** Tasks you're waiting on someone else to complete. Cards you can't act on until a specific date. Anything where "not now" really means "on Friday at 8 AM".

---

## The Show Hidden toggle

The eye icon in the card view header is your temporary window into everything you've set aside. Turn it on and hidden and snoozed cards appear alongside your active cards, slightly faded. Turn it back off and your clean view is restored.

This is useful for a weekly review — toggle it on, look at what you've hidden and snoozed, decide what to restore, then toggle it back off. It's also a good sanity check when you can't find a card you know you created.

The Task List view also respects this toggle. When Show Hidden is on, tasks from hidden and snoozed cards appear in the task table alongside everything else.

---

## Snooze vs. Archive

It's worth knowing when to reach for each one.

- **Snooze** when the card is still active and relevant — you just need it to go away for a while.
- **Hide** when there's no specific time you want it back, but you're not done with it.
- **Archive** (right-click → Archive) when a card is finished and you want to retire it. Archived cards live in Archive View (⌘5) and can be restored if you ever need them.

None of these is permanent. The only permanent action in 3by5 is deleting a card, and even then you get a confirmation prompt.
