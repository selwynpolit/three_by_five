# Views

3by5 gives you five ways to look at your work. Each one is tuned for a different mindset — flip through cards when you're in the flow, switch to the calendar when you're planning ahead, or pull up Today View when you just need to know what's on your plate right now. Switch between them with ⌘1 through ⌘5, or click the icons in the sidebar.

---

## Card View (⌘1)

This is home base. Your cards live here, and there are four ways to look at them — toggled by the icons in the top-left corner of the view.

### Stack View (default)

The leftmost icon gives you the full index-card experience. Your cards are stacked in a draw pile on the left. Flip through them one by one using the left and right arrow keys, or swipe on your trackpad. Cards arc slightly as they move to the discard pile on the right — just like working through a real stack on your desk.

Peek layers show the cards beneath the current one, so you always know there's more to come. The dot indicator at the bottom shows your position in the deck; click any dot to jump straight to that card.

### Sorted Grid (second icon)

All your cards laid out in a wrap grid, newest first. Scroll down to see older cards. Great when you want a bird's-eye view of everything you have going on, or when you're looking for a specific card and can't remember its title.

### Free Canvas (third icon)

Drag cards anywhere on a large open canvas. Pan and zoom with your trackpad. Card positions are saved across sessions, so you can arrange related cards near each other and come back to find them exactly where you left them. This mode is wonderful for project planning — put your "shopping" card next to your "weekend errands" card, or cluster all your work projects together.

### Task List (fourth icon)

The rightmost icon shows every task across every visible card in a single flat table. Columns include: checkbox, priority dot, task title, card name, card date, due date, Now/Later status, tag, and kanban stage. Click any column header to sort — it cycles through ascending, descending, and unsorted. Click again to go to the next state.

There's a search bar at the top. Press ESC to clear it. When you turn on the "Hidden" toggle, tasks from hidden and snoozed cards appear too, which is handy if you're looking for something you set aside a while ago. Click any task row to open its detail panel.

## Kanban View (⌘2)

A classic column board where each column is a stage in your workflow. The default stages are To Do, In Progress, Pending, and Done — but you can rename any of them (double-click the column header) or add new ones with the "+ Add column" button at the right edge.

Drag tasks between columns to move them through your workflow. Drop a task into Done and it's automatically marked complete. Drag it back out and it's un-marked. No clicking required.

Each task card shows a drag handle on hover, the task title, the card it belongs to, and the due date if there is one. Click any task card to open the full detail panel.

The search bar at the top filters tasks by title or card name. The "Hide completed" toggle clears out your Done column when you want a cleaner view.

---

## Calendar View (⌘3)

A month grid showing incomplete tasks that have a due date. Days with tasks are highlighted — click a day and its tasks appear in a list on the right side of the screen. Click any task in that list to open its detail panel.

Task chips in the month grid show as much of the title as fits the cell. When a title is too long to fit, hovering over the chip shows a tooltip with the full title and, if set, the priority and due date below in a smaller muted line.

**Drag to reschedule** — grab a task chip and drop it onto another day to change its due date. The destination day highlights as you drag over it and gives a brief pulse when the task lands. The time of day is preserved — only the date moves. Changed your mind? Press ⌘Z (or the Undo button on the toast) to send it back.

Navigate between months with the ← and → buttons. The "Today" button snaps you back to the current month. The priority colour legend at the bottom shows you what red, blue, and gray dots mean (high, normal, and low priority).

## Today View (⌘4)

Your daily dashboard. Open this in the morning and it tells you exactly where you stand.

Three sections, in order:

- **Overdue** (red) — tasks with due dates that have already passed
- **Today** (orange/amber) — tasks due today
- **Upcoming** (blue) — tasks due in the next seven days

Each task shows its name, the date on its card, and the card's project title so you have context at a glance. When all three sections are empty, you get a small celebration — you've earned it.

Click any task to open its detail panel.

---

## Archive View (⌘5)

Cards you've archived live here. Archiving is the gentle way to retire a card — it's not gone, just put away. The layout is the same grid as Sorted Grid.

Right-click any archived card and choose "Restore" to bring it back into your active Card View. It'll slot right back in as though nothing happened.
