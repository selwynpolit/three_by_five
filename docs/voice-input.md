# Voice Input

The vision for voice input in 3by5 is straightforward: speak a task, and it appears on your current card. Say "remind me Friday" and the due date fills in automatically. Capture a rambling thought as a voice note and come back to polish it later.

The groundwork is in place — the speech-to-text library is installed and wired into the project. But the microphone button hasn't been built yet, so voice input isn't available in the current version.

> **Coming Soon** — Spoken task creation. Say a task aloud and it's added to the current card without touching the keyboard.

> **Coming Soon** — Natural language due dates. Phrases like "remind me Friday", "due next week", or "this afternoon" will be parsed automatically into a due date.

> **Coming Soon** — Voice note capture. Record a quick voice note for any task. Great for capturing thoughts while your hands are busy, or when you're moving too fast to type.

---

## Why it matters

A physical 3×5 card is something you can grab and scribble on without breaking flow. Voice input is the digital equivalent of that — capture first, organise later. The goal is to make getting things out of your head and into 3by5 as frictionless as possible, whatever you're doing when the thought arrives.

When voice input ships, it'll feel like a natural extension of what's already here — not a separate mode, just another way in.
